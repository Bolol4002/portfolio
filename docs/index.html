About.

I'm Sayooj S, an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at KL University, Hyderabad, specializing in VLSI. I design and verify digital hardware — from pipelined processors and custom accelerators to bus protocols — and I get genuinely excited about watching an instruction flow through a pipeline.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware and software: SystemVerilog RTL for CPU microarchitecture, cocotb / UVM-style verification environments for proving designs, and Python tooling for performance analysis and automation. I'm equally happy wiring a simulation in Verilator as I am running synthesis in Vivado.

A strong Linux advocate, I live in terminal-first workflows — scripting builds, parsing logs, and keeping my whole lab reproducible. I've driven a Verilog design all the way from RTL to GDS in OpenLane, and I'm currently researching a configurable AI accelerator for an embedded RISC-V core on FPGA.

I'm looking for hardware verification, RTL design, and VLSI opportunities where I can contribute to real silicon.
