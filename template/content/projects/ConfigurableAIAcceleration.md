Research project to design a configurable MAC-based AI accelerator integrated with a PicoRV32 RISC-V core on the Basys 3 FPGA. Parameterizable MAC array (2/4/8 units), INT8/INT16/INT32 operand widths and custom RISC-V instructions (`MATMUL`, `CONV`, `MAC`). Phases 0–2 complete.
